About

I am Pei-Ying Yu, a student in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Chung Cheng University. I have consistently ranked in the top 4% of my department and have received the Presidential Honor Award four times. I have built a solid foundation in the six core CS courses as well as VLSI Design, and hold a TOEIC score of 915 (Gold-level certificate), demonstrating strong proficiency in reading technical literature in English.

During my undergraduate studies, I joined the Silicon Sensor and System Lab, where I worked under Prof. Jing-Jer Chung on a CNN hardware accelerator for real-time UAV wildfire detection, responsible for RTL architecture design and ASIC backend implementation. Through this work, I came to realize that Edge AI deployment efficiency depends not only on hardware architecture but also on model design itself — which led me to join the Bio-ASP Lab at Academia Sinica, extending my research focus to model compression and edge deployment, where I led the LiteEMG project, distilling the Mamba-based SSEMG-Net teacher model into a lightweight convolutional student via time–frequency response knowledge distillation.

Beyond technical work, I value teamwork and communication. I have served as Public Relations Chair of my department's student association and led a joint inter-departmental celebration, and I continue to volunteer as a tutor for underprivileged students.

Research Experience

  • Jul 2026 – Present Student Research Assistant / Summer Research Intern
    Bio-Mechatronics and Physiological Signal Processing (Bio-ASP) Lab, Academia Sinica (Advisor: Dr. Yu Tsao)
    • Led the LiteEMG project, distilling three time–frequency enhancement responses (magnitude mask, enhanced magnitude spectrogram, enhanced complex spectrogram) from the Mamba-based SSEMG-Net teacher into a lightweight fully convolutional student model.
    • Achieved a 49.1x reduction in model parameters, a 44.9x reduction in FLOPs, and roughly a 260x CPU inference speedup, while retaining 90.5% of the teacher model's denoising performance (SNR improvement).
  • Undergraduate Undergraduate Researcher
    Silicon Sensor and System Lab (Advisor: Prof. Ching-Che Chung)
    • Led the project "Real-Time UAV Wildfire Detection: A Lightweight CNN Hardware Accelerator Based on TSMC N16 FinFET," with responsibility for RTL architecture design and ASIC backend implementation.
    • Led backend logic synthesis and gate-level simulation on the TSMC N16 process, achieving a 300 MHz clock frequency, 183.15–130.65 FPS inference throughput across progressively deeper exit points, a +0.72 ns positive slack, the chip area converges to approximately 305,644.79 µm², and 60.68–77.51 mW power consumption across three exit points, corresponding to a 10–12% reduction in power consumption.
